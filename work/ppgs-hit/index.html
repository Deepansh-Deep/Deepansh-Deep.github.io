Building PPGS at HIT, and making it run without me

Organisation
Public Policy and Governance Society, HIT Haldia
My role
Co-founder, then President
When
May 2025 to present
Type
Leadership
Status
Ongoing

Co-founded the HIT chapter of a society that already ran at IIT Kharagpur, staffed it, funded its events through local sponsorship, became its President, and trained the year below to run it without me.

In 2025, Haldia Institute of Technology had no student body for public policy, debate or structured public speaking. PPGS already existed at IIT Kharagpur. Subid Thakur, then in fourth year, was the main founder of the HIT chapter. I was co-founder, in third year, and took responsibility for building it on the ground.

The first real task was not paperwork. It was persuading the IIT Kharagpur team, with no prior relationship, that a Haldia chapter would run their brand responsibly. Permission was granted, and I recruited roughly fourteen third-year students, assigning roles by what each person was already good at.

About a month in, academic pressure hit and most contributors went quiet at the same time. I ran the society largely on my own for roughly a month, treating that as a temporary fix with a deadline. The permanent fix was to pick specific juniors, teach each of them a task directly and let them own it.

I became President in July 2026. By then the point was already clear: PPGS runs a three-layer workflow across academic years. Second years produce, third years review and manage, and I guide. DRISHTIKON, in September 2026, was run by the team with me as manager and guide rather than as the person doing every task.

A society that only works when the founder is in the room has not been built. It has been performed.

Outcomes

  • Three competitive events delivered, Debatocracy, Voice of Ideas and DRISHTIKON
  • ₹10,000 in cash sponsorship plus one in-kind food deal, both from in-person cold outreach
  • About 150 applicants from an Instagram recruitment reel after classroom access was refused
  • A twelve-member core committee and a three-layer workflow that runs without me

The journey

  1. May 2025

    Co-founded the HIT chapter with Subid Thakur after persuading the IIT Kharagpur team to give permission. Recruited about fourteen third-year students.

  2. 13 Sep 2025

    Debatocracy, the society's first public event.

  3. Late 2025

    Contributors went quiet under academic pressure. Ran the society largely alone for about a month, then trained replacements.

  4. Mar 2026

    Represented HIT alone at Sansad 2K26, IIT Kharagpur. Ran Voice of Ideas with ₹10,000 in cash sponsorship.

  5. 2026

    Recruitment drive moved to Instagram after classroom access was refused. About 150 applied. Structured interviews replaced referrals.

  6. Jul 2026

    Became President.

  7. 19 Sep 2026

    DRISHTIKON, run by the team, with me as manager, guide and judge.

  8. Now

    The society runs without my day-to-day involvement. I guide; the team decides and delivers.

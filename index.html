04 About

I'd rather be in the room than behind the screen

I'm a B.Tech student in Computer Science (Data Science) at Haldia Institute of Technology, graduating in 2027. I started as a coder. What I actually enjoy is people: running events, building communities and getting a message to the right audience.

I've sold in my family's jewellery shop in Bokaro since 2021, which taught me more about trust and negotiation than any course. Since then I've co-founded a student society and become its President, grown from awareness member to Awareness Head at an animal welfare NGO, and done marketing work for local businesses. Both organisations now run without me directing every step.

“A society that only works when the founder is in the room has not been built. It has been performed.”

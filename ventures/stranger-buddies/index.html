05Booking and cancellation

Book now, decide by December

Hotels and train tickets have to be booked in advance, so the policy gets stricter as the trip gets closer.

  1. Now to 1 December 2026 Book with ₹200 Your ₹200 booking amount holds your seat. Change your mind before 1 December and it is refunded in full.
  2. 1 December 2026 Confirmation email Everyone who booked gets a confirmation email. To keep your seat, you pay 100% of your package in advance. From this date the ₹200 is no longer refundable, because hotels and train tickets are booked ahead.
  3. Up to the first week of January 2027 Medical emergencies only After full payment, a booking can only be cancelled for a medical emergency that stops you from travelling. Tell the team by the first week of January 2027 so the tickets can be cancelled in time.
  4. After the first week of January 2027 No cancellations The booking can no longer be cancelled.

07For a safe, fun and unforgettable trip

The rules

  1. 01 Be respectfulRespect everyone's space, choices and boundaries.
  2. 02 Safety firstFollow the coordinators' instructions and stay with the group.
  3. 03 Be on timeStick to the schedule and be ready before departure times.
  4. 04 No alcohol, no drugsStrictly no alcohol, drugs or illegal substances.
  5. 05 Be inclusiveTalk to new people and make sure no one feels left out.
  6. 06 Keep places cleanNo litter on the beaches, at temples or in the hotel.
  7. 07 Meals and expensesBreakfast is included. Lunch and dinner are paid by participants. Carry extra cash.
  8. 08 Respect hotel rulesLook after the property. Any damage is the responsible person’s to pay for.
  9. 09 Dress appropriatelyComfortable clothes for travel and beach. Dress modestly at temples.
  10. 10 Stay with the groupDon't wander off alone. Tell a coordinator if you need to step away.
  11. 11 Capture, don’t disturbTake photos, but ask before posting anyone's photo or video.
  12. 12 Be kind and supportiveHelp each other. Good vibes only.
  13. 13 No smoking in closed spacesNot in buses, hotel rooms or any closed area.
  14. 14 Follow the coordinatorsThe Strangers Buddies team makes the final call on the itinerary and any changes.

08Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know anyone on the trip?

No. The trip is built for people who are meeting for the first time. That is the whole idea.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 18 to 25.

What does the ₹200 booking do?

It holds your seat. Cancel before 1 December 2026 and you get the ₹200 back. From 1 December it is not refundable.

When do I pay the full amount?

After the confirmation email on 1 December 2026. You pay 100% of your package in advance to keep your seat.

Can I cancel after paying in full?

Only for a medical emergency that stops you from travelling, and only if you tell the team by the first week of January 2027. After that, bookings cannot be cancelled.

Which meals are included?

Breakfast every morning. Lunch and dinner, including the Mahaprasad dinner, are at your own expense, so carry some extra cash.

Is alcohol allowed?

No. Alcohol, drugs and illegal substances are strictly not allowed on the trip.

Ascendant

In progress

Product scope is being defined. No public version yet. Everything below is a preview.

Fitness tracking appProgress you can see, not just feel.

A gym progress tracker for people who want to see, in data, whether their training is actually working.

Ascendant is an app for tracking gym progress. Most people who train have no reliable record of whether they’re improving. Ascendant is meant to record workouts, measure outcomes and show progress through dashboards, so the answer comes from data instead of the mirror.

How it's meant to work

The intended flow. Screens will be shown here once they exist.

  1. 1

    Log the session

    Record each workout as you train, so progress is written down instead of remembered.

  2. 2

    Measure the outcome

    Your logged sessions become measurements over time.

  3. 3

    Review performance

    Look back week by week at what improved and what stalled.

  4. 4

    Read the dashboard

    See your progress as charts and fitness data rather than a list of numbers.

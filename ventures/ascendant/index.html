Ascendant

In progress

Pricing is decided. It is not on sale yet, and the launch date isn't set.

Workout tracker (spreadsheet)Progress you can see, not just feel.

A spreadsheet workout tracker for your gym splits and progression. ₹19 for the tracker, ₹100 for Pro, which adds membership of Beyond Hello.

Ascendant is a workout tracker built as a spreadsheet. Most people who train have no reliable record of whether they are improving. Ascendant records your split, your sessions and your numbers, and shows the progression, so the answer comes from data instead of the mirror.

It is a product, not a company: one thing you buy and use. Ascendant Pro adds membership of Beyond Hello, the community I’m building.

Not confirmed yet

  • Launch date

How it works

The tracker is a spreadsheet, so it works on any phone or laptop that opens Excel or Google Sheets.

  1. 1

    Set your split

    Choose the days and the muscle groups you train, once.

  2. 2

    Log every session

    Sets, reps and weight, written down as you train instead of remembered.

  3. 3

    Watch the progression

    The sheet turns your logs into progression, exercise by exercise.

  4. 4

    Review the week

    See what moved up, what stalled and what to change.

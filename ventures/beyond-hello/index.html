Beyond Hello

Concept

A concept. No sign-in, chat or video exists yet.

Invite-only community

An invite-only community for my followers and people I admire. Members get a login issued by the admin, then chat and video call each other.

Beyond Hello is a private space for people I want around me: followers, people I admire, and people who ask to join and are approved. It is a community first, and the app is just the room it happens in.

Membership is by invitation. The admin issues each member a login, so nobody without one can get in. Before it opens, it needs proper authentication, moderation and a way to report people, especially for video calls. None of that exists yet, and this site does not pretend it does.

Not confirmed yet

  • Moderation, reporting and rules for video calls between members

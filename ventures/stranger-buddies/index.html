Strangersbuddies

Upcoming

The first trip and the first Chaos Call are being planned.

Travel and social experiences

Bringing people who don't know each other together through shared experiences, on the road and online.

Strangersbuddies is built on one bet: people who have never met can have a better time together than people who already know each other, if someone designs the experience well.

It runs two kinds of experience. Trips take a group of strangers somewhere together. Chaos Call is a free online event where strangers meet on a video call for games, dares and conversation.

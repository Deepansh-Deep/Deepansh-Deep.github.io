Three days, hour by hour

Day 01 Arrival, relax, beach and poolFriday, 12 February 2027
  1. 10:00 AM Arrive at Bhubaneswar Reach Bhubaneswar by train.
  2. 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM Road trip to Puri Two to two and a half hours of scenic drive, with music, singing and time to get to know each other. Breakfast by Strangers Buddies
  3. 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM Check in and rest Reach Puri, check in to the hotel and rest for two hours.
  4. 2:00 PM Lunch At your own expense.
  5. Evening Niladri Beach Beach walks, sunset views and group time.
  6. Night Pool party Music, poolside vibes and new friendships. Included
  7. Optional Spa Relax at the hotel spa, at extra cost. Extra charge
Day 02 Temples, beaches, markets and game nightSaturday, 13 February 2027
  1. 7:00 AM to 8:30 AM Breakfast A fresh start at the hotel. Breakfast by Strangers Buddies
  2. 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM Jagannath Temple darshan Visit the sacred Shree Jagannath Temple.
  3. 12:30 PM to 3:00 PM Lunch and rest Lunch at your own expense, then rest at the hotel.
  4. 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM Beach exploration Blue Flag Beach, Golden Beach, the Puri beach market, and water activities if possible.
  5. 7:00 PM Back to the hotel Return and freshen up.
  6. 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM Mahaprasad dinner The traditional Puri Mahaprasad, together. At your own expense.
  7. 9:00 PM to 11:00 PM Game night Truth and dare, dumb charades, fun games and endless conversations.
Day 03 Explore, return, carry the memoriesSunday, 14 February 2027
  1. 8:00 AM to 8:30 AM Breakfast At the hotel. Breakfast by Strangers Buddies
  2. 8:30 AM Leave Puri Check out and head back towards Bhubaneswar.
  3. 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM Chandrabhaga Beach A short stop of 15 to 30 minutes at the beach.
  4. 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM Hills and caves Explore the scenic hills and ancient caves. Optional
  5. 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM Konark Sun Temple The UNESCO World Heritage Site and its history.

  10. Next day Arrive at Bokaro Home, with new friends and a lot of stories.

For a safe, fun and unforgettable trip

The rules

  1. 01 Be respectful Respect everyone's space, choices and boundaries.
  2. 02 Safety first Follow the coordinators' instructions and stay with the group.
  3. 03 Be on time Stick to the schedule and be ready before departure times.
  4. 04 No alcohol, no drugs Strictly no alcohol, drugs or illegal substances.
  5. 05 Be inclusive Talk to new people and make sure no one feels left out.
  6. 06 Keep places clean No litter on the beaches, at temples or in the hotel.
  7. 07 Meals and expenses Breakfast is included. Lunch and dinner are paid by participants. Carry extra cash.
  8. 08 Respect hotel rules Look after the property. Any damage is the responsible person’s to pay for.
  9. 09 Dress appropriately Comfortable clothes for travel and beach. Dress modestly at temples.
  10. 10 Stay with the group Don't wander off alone. Tell a coordinator if you need to step away.
  11. 11 Capture, don’t disturb Take photos, but ask before posting anyone's photo or video.
  12. 12 Be kind and supportive Help each other. Good vibes only.
  13. 13 No smoking in closed spaces Not in buses, hotel rooms or any closed area.
  14. 14 Follow the coordinators The Strangers Buddies team makes the final call on the itinerary and any changes.

From awareness member to Awareness Head at Bezubaan

Organisation
Bezubaan, HIT Haldia
My role
Awareness member, then Awareness Head
When
Apr 2024 to present
Type
Community
Status
Ongoing

Bezubaan is a student-led NGO for animal welfare. I joined its awareness team in 2024, ran my first campaign that August, and now lead the team, which runs its campaigns without me directing every step.

Awareness work means getting people to care about animals they walk past every day. I started by doing it myself: promoting campaigns and getting volunteers to show up.

As Awareness Head, the job changed. It’s no longer about how many drives I personally run. It’s whether the team can plan and deliver one without me, and today it can.

Outcomes

  • Promoted 5 to 7 awareness campaigns
  • Recruited, briefed and coordinated volunteer teams for on-ground outreach
  • Lead member of the fundraising committee, running public collection drives

Beyond Hello

Concept

A future concept. No sign-in, voice or video chat exists yet.

Community platform concept

A proposed voice and video chat space for approved members of the community, without a Gmail login.

Beyond Hello is an idea for a private space where people from the community can actually talk to each other, by voice or video, instead of only following the same accounts.

Membership would be by approval, with each member given their own ID rather than signing in through Gmail. If it is built, accounts will use secure authentication, protected credentials, access controls and privacy safeguards. None of that exists yet, and this site does not pretend it does.

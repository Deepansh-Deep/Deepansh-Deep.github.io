About

I'm Deepansh. I grew up in Bokaro Steel City in Jharkhand and study Computer Science (Data Science) at Haldia Institute of Technology in West Bengal, graduating in 2027.

I came in as a coder. Somewhere between running the gaming department at my school fest in class 8 and organising my locality's Saraswati Puja, I noticed the part I actually enjoyed was never the code. It was the people: getting them to show up, getting them to care, and making the day work when it doesn't go to plan.

So that's what I do now. I co-founded the Public Policy and Governance Society at HIT in 2025 and became its President in July 2026. I joined Bezubaan, a student-led animal welfare NGO, as an awareness member in 2024 and became its Awareness Head in June 2026. I've done PR for ISTE, marketing work for a gym, and I've just started UTSAB Club in Jaina More for esports and culture.

The thing I'm proudest of isn't any single event. It's that PPGS and Bezubaan's awareness team both run today without me directing every step. I built the people and the routines, then stepped back.

I've also worked in my family's jewellery shop since 2021. Selling something worth tens of thousands of rupees to someone who doesn't know you teaches you trust, patience and negotiation faster than anything else I've done.

A society that only works when the founder is in the room has not been built. It has been performed.

What I'm working towards

A career in events, experiential marketing and community building, and eventually my own events company. I want to learn professional event planning, budgeting, sponsorship, vendors and logistics first, from people who do it well.

Outside work

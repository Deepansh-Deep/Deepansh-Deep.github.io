Chaos Call

Upcoming

Free online social event

A free online event where strangers meet on Zoom or Google Meet for games, dares and conversation.

Chaos Call is free and online. Strangers join one Zoom or Google Meet call and play: games, truth or dare, surprise challenges, secret tasks, funny memories and some light roasting. There are prizes.

Registration and the WhatsApp group will open once the first date is set. Past calls and their photos will appear on this page after they happen.

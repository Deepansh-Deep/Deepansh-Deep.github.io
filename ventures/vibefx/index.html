VibeFX

Live

Version 2.4, the official release. Works in Chrome and Edge for video calls in the browser. Free for everyone.

Chrome and Edge extensionYour hands are the remote.

Gesture-triggered effects for video calls in your browser. Wave and you turn to dust; raise both hands and you're on a film poster. Eleven effects, your own memes, and a Freeze button for when someone walks in.

VibeFX watches for hand gestures on your webcam and answers them with visual reactions on your video call. It’s built to be fun first: something you use because it makes the room laugh. It also has one genuinely useful trick: Freeze, for the moment someone walks in behind you.

It runs entirely in your browser, on your own computer. The illustrations further down show how the headline effects look; real recordings will replace them.

How to get it running

  1. 1

    Download it

    Press Download on this page. It's free, no sign-up. You get one zip file (26 MB).

  2. 2

    Add it to Chrome or Edge

    Extract the zip. Open chrome://extensions (Edge, edge://extensions), switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the VibeFX folder. Keep the folder where it is.

  3. 3

    Open your call

    Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord, Whereby, Webex, Jitsi or Slack huddles, in the browser. If the tab was already open, reload it once and allow the camera.

  4. 4

    Make a gesture

    Palm towards the camera, hand clearly in view, good light. The effect plays inside your own video, so everyone on the call sees it.

What it does with your camera and mic

  • Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
  • Your microphone is never listened to or recorded. Freeze only switches it off and on.
  • With no effect running, your camera goes straight through, untouched.

Questions

Does it work in the Zoom or Teams desktop app?

No. It works for calls in the browser only (Chrome or Edge, version 116 or later).

Why does it need Developer mode?

It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so for now it installs from a folder. Chrome reads it from that folder, so don't move or delete it.

Will it slow my call down?

With no effect on, it adds no delay. Effects use some processing power; the lighting fix and background blur use clearly more, which is why they start switched off.

A gesture fires when I'm only talking with my hands. What do I do?

Set the sensitivity to Careful, move that effect to another pose, or press Alt+G to pause gestures.

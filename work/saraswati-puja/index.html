Saraswati Puja for the neighbourhood

Organisation
My locality, Bokaro
My role
Organiser
When
2021
Type
Event
Status
Completed

Organised the locality's Saraswati Puja in class 11, on a budget of about ₹13,000 raised from household contributions.

This was the first event I ran with real money and real expectations from the people who paid for it. Every household that contributed would be there on the day.

Outcomes

  • About ₹13,000 raised from households, ₹500 to ₹1,000 each
  • Arranged the murti, vendors, food and prasad, ending with a late-night community dinner

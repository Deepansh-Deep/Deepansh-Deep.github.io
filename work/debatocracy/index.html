Debatocracy

Organisation
Public Policy and Governance Society, HIT Haldia
My role
Organiser
When
13 Sep 2025
Type
Event
Status
Completed

The society's first public event. A debate competition with faculty judges, held in the Annex Building at HIT, three months after the chapter was approved.

Topics were released 25 minutes before each round, positions and opponents were assigned randomly, and rounds ran for 15 minutes of argument and rebuttal.

Registration didn’t convert into attendance. The options were to postpone, cancel, or run a smaller competition than advertised. We ran it. A cancelled first event would have told the campus PPGS couldn’t deliver, and would have wasted the time of two faculty judges. A small event that happened produced photographs, a published record, three winners and a faculty relationship.

Outcomes

  • About 100 students reached in promotion, about 25 registrations at ₹9
  • 13 competed on the day, judged by two faculty members, three winners declared
  • In-kind food sponsorship from a newly opened local food business

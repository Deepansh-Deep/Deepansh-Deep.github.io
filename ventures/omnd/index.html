Marketing agencyObserve. Make. Disrupt.

A digital marketing and business growth agency co-founded by Om Kumar and Deepansh Deep. Instagram, websites, creators, campaigns and strategy, planned as one system.

OM&D goes beyond posting and running ads. Most small businesses we meet have the same problems: posting in bursts with no plan, a website that’s slow or dated on a phone, campaigns with no clear goal, and content, ads and objectives that don’t connect.

Our approach is to plan them together. When strategy, content, website and campaigns are one system, each piece supports the next, and every effort is tied to a business objective.

OM&D is a joint venture between Om Kumar and Deepansh Deep. Client work and case studies will be published here only when they exist and the client has agreed.
